CUBETERRA

An open voxel sandbox. Explore five biomes, mine iron and gold, smelt in a furnace, craft tools and a shield, hunt creatures, sleep in a bed and survive the Gloomlings at night. In Creative you build freely.

Caves, the Nether and the End

Nether: build a frame of Voidglass 4 wide and 5 tall (Voidglass = 2 Coalite + Glass), stand inside and right-click the frame with a Fire Charge (Iron Ingot + Coalite). Step into the purple portal and wait a few seconds.
End: collect Void Pearls (Voidwalkers) and Flame Rods (Flamelings), craft Void Eyes, ring a 3x3 hole with 12 Gate Frames (craft one from Voidglass) and right-click each with an Eye. Defeat the Void Wyrm to open the way home.
Caves: look for ravines and big caverns. Mind the lava below and bring light: it is truly dark down there.

Multiplayer

There is no game server. Players on the same device or browser find each other automatically; friends online connect directly (peer to peer) by swapping two copy-and-paste codes. The host's world is shared; creatures and containers are not synced.
